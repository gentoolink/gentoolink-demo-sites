Meet Your Guide

Mike Sawatsky

Lead Guide & Owner — 18 Years on Northern BC Waters

Born and raised in Vanderhoof, Mike has spent his entire life on the Nechako River and the lakes of Northern BC. He's not just a fishing guide — he's a conservationist, a storyteller, and the guy who knows exactly which rock the big rainbow is hiding under.

Mike holds a BC Freshwater Fishing Guide certificate and carries full liability insurance. His reputation for putting clients on fish — no matter the conditions — has built a loyal following who return year after year.

  • BC Freshwater Fishing Guide License
  • First Aid & Wilderness Safety Certified
  • Catch & Release Conservation Advocate
  • Member — BC Fishing Guide Association
