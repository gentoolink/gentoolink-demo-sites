Who We Are

Following Christ Together in Vanderhoof

Vanderhoof Christian Church has been a home for faith, fellowship, and service in the Bulkley Valley for over 40 years. We believe church isn't a building — it's a family.

Whether you're exploring Christianity for the first time, returning after time away, or looking for a church home, there's a place for you here.

✝
Grace — We believe in a God who loves unconditionally, and we try to reflect that in everything we do.
🤝
Community — We grow best together. From potlucks to service projects, we do life side by side.
🌱
Hope — In a world that can feel uncertain, we hold onto the promise of something greater.
