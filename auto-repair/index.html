Our Story

Vanderhoof Auto Care

For over 25 years, Vanderhoof Auto Care has been the trusted name in automotive repair across Northern BC. Founded in 1998 by long-time mechanic Hank Sievers, our shop grew from a two-bay garage into a full-service auto care centre staffed by a team of five certified technicians.

We believe in doing the job right the first time. That means clear communication, honest advice, and quality parts — whether you're coming in for an oil change or a complete engine overhaul.
