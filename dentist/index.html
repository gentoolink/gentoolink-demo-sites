What We Offer

Comprehensive Dental Services

From preventive care to cosmetic enhancements, our experienced team provides a full range of dental treatments to keep your smile healthy and beautiful.

Cleanings & Exams

Thorough professional cleanings and comprehensive exams to maintain your oral health and catch issues early.

Fillings

Tooth-colored composite fillings that restore strength and appearance. Mercury-free, durable, and natural-looking.

Extractions

Gentle tooth extractions including wisdom teeth. We prioritize your comfort with local anesthesia and sedation options.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Whitening, veneers, and bonding to enhance your smile. Transform your confidence with personalized cosmetic treatments.

Emergency Care

Same-day emergency appointments available. Toothache, broken tooth, or dental trauma — we make room for you quickly.

Root Canals

Painless root canal therapy to save infected teeth. Modern techniques ensure your comfort throughout the procedure.

Meet Our Team

Experienced Dentists You Can Trust

Our dedicated team of dental professionals combines advanced training with a gentle, caring approach to give you the best possible experience.

Dr. Sarah Mitchell

DDS — General & Cosmetic Dentistry

Dr. Mitchell graduated from UBC Faculty of Dentistry with over 12 years of experience in family and cosmetic dentistry. She is known for her gentle approach and commitment to patient education.

Specialties: Cosmetic bonding, teeth whitening, preventive care

Dr. James Nguyen

DMD — General Dentistry & Oral Surgery

Dr. Nguyen brings 8 years of experience in general dentistry with a special interest in oral surgery and complex extractions. He completed his DMD at the University of Manitoba.

Specialties: Extractions, wisdom teeth, emergency dental care

Dr. Emily Park

DDS — Pediatric & Family Dentistry

Dr. Park specializes in making dental visits enjoyable for children and families. With 6 years of pediatric experience, she creates a calm, fun environment for young patients.

Specialties: Children's dentistry, preventive care, sealants

Why Vanderhoof Family Dental
